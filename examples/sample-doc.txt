Product Strategy Update — Q4 2024
Prepared by: Head of Product
Date: November 1, 2024

EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

After a strong Q3 (ARR grew 22% QoQ), the product team is prioritizing three strategic themes for Q4 and into 2025:
enterprise readiness, AI feature differentiation, and platform extensibility.

THEME 1: ENTERPRISE READINESS

We have 14 enterprise prospects in active pipeline representing $3.8M in
potential ARR. Key blockers identified from 9 lost deals this year:
  - SOC 2 Type II certification (in progress, expected January 2025)
  - SSO / SAML support (planned for v3.1, December release)
  - Audit logging (in scope for v3.1)
  - Role-based access control enhancements (v3.2, February 2025)

THEME 2: AI FEATURE DIFFERENTIATION

Competitive analysis shows three competitors shipping AI-assisted features.
Our AI summarization beta has 1,200 active users with 78% weekly retention —
significantly above our product average of 61%. We plan to GA the feature
in November and begin charging for AI credits in January.

Key risk: model API costs are currently running at $0.018 per document.
At projected scale (500K documents/month), this represents $9K/month in
COGS. The team is evaluating caching strategies to reduce this by ~40%.

THEME 3: PLATFORM EXTENSIBILITY

A webhook system and public API v2 are planned for Q1 2025. Early feedback
from 6 design partners suggests this unblocks integration with Salesforce,
HubSpot, and Slack — the top-requested integrations.

RISKS AND MITIGATIONS

1. SOC 2 delay: auditor has flagged two control gaps. Legal is addressing.
   Target remains January but a 4-6 week slip is possible.
2. Engineering capacity: two senior engineers are on medical leave through
   November. PM is de-scoping 3 lower-priority items to compensate.
